# Privacy Policy for WHOOP Analyzer

Last updated: September 17, 2026

WHOOP Analyzer is a personal health and fitness analytics project that integrates with the WHOOP API.

## Data Access

With the user's authorization, WHOOP Analyzer may access:

- Recovery data, including recovery score, heart rate variability (HRV), and resting heart rate
- Cycle data, including day strain
- Sleep data, including sleep performance and related sleep metrics

## How Data Is Used

The data is used solely to provide personal health, recovery, sleep, and training analytics to the authorized user.

## Data Storage

WHOOP data may be stored locally on the user's device for personal analysis. Personal WHOOP data is not intentionally stored in this project's public GitHub repository.

## Data Sharing

WHOOP Analyzer does not sell personal data or share personal WHOOP data with third parties.

## Authorization

Access to WHOOP data requires the user's explicit authorization through WHOOP's OAuth process. Users may revoke access through their WHOOP account.

## Contact

For privacy-related questions, please use the contact information associated with the WHOOP Developer application.
